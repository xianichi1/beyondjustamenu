Beyond Just a Menu
Beyond Just a Menu is a graphic design thesis project exploring the disconnect between public consumption of Asian culture (food, trends, pop culture) and the ongoing realities of anti-Asian racism in Toronto.

Project Structure
Street Poster Campaign: High-impact physical posters utilizing a torn-paper aesthetic. The visual layer tears away a hospitable smile to reveal a face mask printed with the text: "WE ARE MORE THAN JUST A COMMODITY."

Sticker Interventions: "Clickbait" stickers placed around street infrastructure (utility posts, transit stops) featuring prompts like "Scan for Exclusive Drop" or "Scan for Free Boba".

Digital Component: Interactive QR codes on physical touchpoints leading directly to local anti-racism data, news articles, and an anonymous community message board.

Process Book: Documentation covering fieldwork photography, typography exploration, vector graphics, and physical mockups.
